# Cox lc if X CY type MDS

Theorem 1.3. Let (X, ) be a projective Q-factorial log Calabi–Yau pair over C such that X is a Mori Dream Space, and let D1, . . . , Dr be a basis for the torsion free part of Cl(X). Then the ring Cox(X; D1, . . . , Dr ) is normal with log canonical singularities. <a href="zotero://open/library/items/MX5GNADP?page=2&#x26;annotation=YFKDNLWG">(page)</a></a> (<a href="zotero://select/library/items/6SLBPKDC">Brown, 2013, p. 656</a>)
